# Purpose

> One page. Everything else in this repo cites this.

## Mission

Tarun Shukla runs technology, growth and operations for businesses that can't hire a department — and proves it with numbers.

The site exists to turn that sentence into two outcomes:

1. **A role.** Head of Digital / Technology, AI-native full-stack lead, or founding engineer at a UAE SME, agency or startup, at AED 18,000+/month.
2. **Clients.** SME owners who need a Next.js site, SEO that ranks, Zoho that works, and AI/WhatsApp/n8n automation — bought as one bundle from one person.

## Audiences, ranked

| | Who | What they decide in 30 seconds | What convinces them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Founders, GMs, heads of HR at UAE SMEs / family groups / agencies / funded startups; recruiters sent by them | "Can this person own our whole digital stack, not just write code?" | Outcome numbers (9.2× ROAS, AED 242K revenue from AED 26K ads, AED 2.27M pipeline, AED 160K saved, 776,478 stock units corrected), 7 products shipped in 8 months, Lighthouse 95–100 on every one, team leadership, ERP ownership |
| **B** | Owners of trading / distribution / contracting / retail SMEs in the UAE | "Has he done this for a business like mine, and will it pay for itself?" | Case studies with before/after, real screenshots, the lapizblue.com story (1,200 → 62 pages, rankings kept, #1s), a clear service bundle, a working contact path |

A hiring manager skims on a phone in seconds; an SME owner reads one case study end-to-end. The site serves both: a 5-second hero, 90-second case studies.

## Positioning

**"Head of Digital & Technology for SMEs"** first, **"AI-native full-stack engineer"** second. Never "junior developer", never "Zoho developer", never "SEO freelancer" — those titles cap the salary band and crowd the search results (see `research/03` and `research/04`).

Tagline direction: *I build the tech, growth and operations engine for companies that can't hire a department.*

## Primary conversions

- A: open ≥ 1 case study → download the ATS résumé or send a message. Success metric: ≥ 2 qualified conversations per quarter.
- B: open a service page → read ≥ 1 case study → send an enquiry. Success metric: ≥ 2 qualified enquiries per quarter.

## Non-goals

- Not a blog-first site (writing is optional, later, and only where there is zero competition).
- Not a template, not a component showcase, not a Three.js demo.
- Not a job-board SEO play ("full stack developer Dubai" is job-ad intent).
- Not a clone of phantom.land or any reference site — we borrow mechanics, never pixels, copy or assets.

## Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Every number is sourced (`src/content/**` cites resume / deliverables sheet / Halfyearly-Report). No superlatives.
2. **One orchestrated motion moment per page.** Everything else is a response to the user's action.
3. **Fast on a phone.** Lighthouse 100/100/100/100 on every route, mobile and desktop, is a release gate — not a target.
4. **Nothing fabricated.** No invented clients, metrics, testimonials or logos. Placeholders are labelled.
5. **Never desperate.** No "hire me" in the nav, no "open to work" banner, no urgency copy. Availability is stated once, calmly, on the contact page.
6. **Accessible by default.** Reduced-motion parity, keyboard access for every interaction, ≥ 24 px targets, AA contrast.

## Constraints

- Tarun is employed; freelance availability copy waits for a permit/NOC (see `research/03`).
- The bare name "Tarun Shukla" is contested in search by a published author; we target "Tarun Shukla Dubai / developer" and long-tail service queries (see `research/04`).
- Vercel Hobby limits (image transformations) are comfortable for a portfolio; third-party scripts are limited to Vercel Analytics.
